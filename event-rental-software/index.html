Give the customer a common reference.

A list of equipment does not always explain how an event will feel. A drawing adds context: where guests sit, where service happens and how the central space is used. RentSketch turns the conversation into a plan the customer can explore.

Create your business workspace.

Start a trial, add your products and configure your branding. Each product can carry dimensions, pricing and a supported visual mapping. The business dashboard brings customer designs and quote requests into the same workspace.

Install the customer experience.

You can embed your designer on your existing rental website after configuring its allowed domain, or share a direct link. Review the customer journey yourself: opening the designer, selecting equipment and submitting a request should match how your business discusses an event.

Treat quote requests as requests.

A submitted design does not guarantee availability or make a final booking. Review the plan, confirm inventory and check the event location before agreeing on the rental. Deposit and integration workflows require their own setup; they are not automatically connected to every rental platform.

Check fit before you subscribe.

The trial lasts 14 days and does not require a card. Use that time to compare the supported visuals and workflow with the equipment and process you use. When you are ready, open Billing and review the plan and interval in Stripe before paying.
