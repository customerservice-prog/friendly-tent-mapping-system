Event rental software should give customers a common reference.

A list of rentals rarely explains the whole event. RentSketch lets customers arrange tents, seating, dance floors and supported equipment in one visual plan so the rental team can discuss the same picture.

Build a catalog around the equipment you actually rent.

Configure names, categories, prices, dimensions, photos and supported visual mappings. The designer uses that catalog for customer planning and the business dashboard keeps submitted layouts and quote requests attached to the rental-company workspace.

Use it with your existing event rental management software.

RentSketch focuses on visual planning and quote requests. It does not currently replace full reservation inventory, dispatch, route scheduling, accounting or contract systems. Keep those operational systems authoritative and use RentSketch to improve the layout and quote conversation.

Searching for “rent event registration software”?

If you mean attendee registration, ticketing or check-in, RentSketch is not that category of software. RentSketch is for rental-product and physical event-layout planning. If your business needs guest registration, keep that in your registration platform and use RentSketch for the physical event setup.
