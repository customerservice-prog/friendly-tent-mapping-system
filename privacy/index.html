Privacy at RentSketch.

Information used to provide the event design service.

Account and event information

RentSketch stores business account information, including the business name, email address and a hashed password. The service also stores event designs and the information submitted with quote requests so users and the relevant rental business can work with those plans.

Payments and access

Stripe hosts checkout and handles payment information. RentSketch stores payment references, payment status, the purchase email and access periods to provide subscriptions or Event Pass access. Private event links are used to reopen a purchased design.

Browser storage

The designer uses browser storage for draft and session information. The business dashboard stores a sign-in token so the account can remain signed in. Signing out clears the dashboard token; clearing browser storage can remove local drafts and preview state.

Service providers and rental businesses

Hosting, payment and email services process information needed to operate RentSketch. When you use a rental company’s designer and submit an event or quote request, that business receives the relevant information. Its handling of rental orders is separate from RentSketch software access.

Questions and requests

Contact hello@rentsketch.com with privacy, account or data requests. Include enough information to identify your account, but never send a password, full payment card number or private event access link.
