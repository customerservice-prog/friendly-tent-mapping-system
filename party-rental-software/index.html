Solve the layout questions earlier.

Customers often know the occasion and guest count before they know the equipment. A visual layout lets you discuss how dining, dancing and service areas will work together. RentSketch focuses on those layout conversations so a quote can begin with a clearer picture.

Connect the design to your catalog.

Add the names, dimensions and prices of the equipment you rent. Supported visual mappings make items recognizable in the designer. Check the catalog during your trial; not every unusual rental item automatically has a custom 3D model.

Use your website as the starting point.

Configure your branding and add the approved website domain in the Install screen. An embed brings the designer into your existing site, while a direct designer link gives you another way to share it. Preview the customer experience before you publish it.

Keep the rental decisions in your workflow.

RentSketch supplies layouts and quote requests. It does not promise automatic inventory availability, dispatch scheduling or a connection to every booking system. Confirm stock, delivery, installation and final rental terms through your existing process.

Evaluate it with a real planning scenario.

Use the 14-day business trial to configure several common products, build a representative event and review a quote request. Compare the customer view with the information your team needs. No credit card is required, and choosing a trial plan does not charge you automatically.
