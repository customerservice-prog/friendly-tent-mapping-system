Party rental business software for the customer-planning step.

RentSketch gives party and event rental businesses a branded 2D and 3D event designer, a configurable rental-product catalog, saved customer layouts and quote requests. It is built for the visual planning step that often happens before a rental team finalizes availability, delivery and installation details.

Party rental inventory software starts with an accurate product catalog.

Add rental product names, categories, dimensions, prices, photos and supported visual mappings so customers plan with recognizable equipment. RentSketch does not replace live stock-count or reservation software, so confirm actual inventory, date availability and holds in your booking system before accepting an order.

Party rental management software should fit the operation you already run.

Use RentSketch beside your existing booking, delivery and accounting workflow. The business dashboard keeps products, customer designs and quote requests together, while your rental system remains authoritative for reservations, contracts, route scheduling and final payments unless you configure a supported integration.
