Party rental business software for the customer-planning step.

RentSketch gives party and event rental businesses a branded 2D and 3D event designer, a configurable rental-product catalog, saved customer layouts and quote requests. It is built for the visual planning step that often happens before a rental team finalizes availability, delivery and installation details.

Party rental inventory software starts with accurate availability and a clear catalog.

Customers need recognizable products; operators need trustworthy quantities and date availability. RentSketch handles the visual catalog—names, categories, dimensions, prices, photos and supported visual mappings—but does not replace live stock-count or reservation software. See the party rental inventory software guide for the operational features to keep authoritative.

Party rental management software should fit the operation you already run.

Use RentSketch beside your existing booking, delivery and accounting workflow. The business dashboard keeps products, customer designs and quote requests together, while your rental system remains authoritative for reservations, contracts, route scheduling and final payments unless you configure a supported integration. The party rental management software guide breaks down the full workflow.

Party and event rental software needs a customer-facing experience.

Install the branded designer on an approved website domain or share a direct designer link. Customers can work through the visual questions before they call: tent footprint, seating, dance floor and other equipment. That gives your staff a clearer starting point for the quote conversation.
