Show customers the tent before the quote becomes a spreadsheet.

A tent rental often begins with a guest count and a size question. RentSketch lets customers start with a supported tent model, add tables, chairs and a dance floor, and review the same event in 2D and 3D before your team finalizes the rental.

Build a tent catalog with real dimensions and prices.

Rental businesses can configure product names, categories, dimensions, prices, photos and supported visual mappings. Pole and frame tent visuals can give customers a clearer picture of the footprint and interior planning conversation.

Use RentSketch beside tent inventory and scheduling software.

RentSketch does not currently replace component-level tent inventory, crew scheduling, route planning or maintenance systems. Keep your operational tent-rental software authoritative for poles, stakes, sidewalls, availability and installation logistics. The inventory software guide explains those controls in more detail.

Bring customer layouts into the quote workflow.

A submitted plan can give your staff useful context before calling the customer: the selected tent, seating idea, dance-floor placement and other supported rentals. Treat the plan as a request, then verify the site, inventory and installation requirements before booking.

Use the 14-day business trial without a credit card. Add one or two common tents and supporting rentals, preview the branded customer experience and review a test request before deciding whether to subscribe.
