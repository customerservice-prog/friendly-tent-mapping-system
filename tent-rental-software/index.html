Tent rental software should reflect how tent companies actually sell an event.

A tent quote is not just a roof size. Customers need to understand the footprint, seating, center poles or frame structure, dance floor, service areas and the clear space around the installation. RentSketch adds a visual planning layer so that conversation starts with a picture instead of a list.

Show the tent first, then build the event around it.

For product-level browsing, a clean tent photo is often the fastest answer to “what does this tent look like?” Full event planning belongs in the event designer, where the customer can arrange seating and other equipment after deciding to plan an event.

Keep inventory, engineering and installation authority outside the drawing.

The layout does not approve anchoring, staking, ballast, egress or site conditions. Your operational system and installation process remain authoritative for availability, components, delivery, permits and manufacturer requirements.
