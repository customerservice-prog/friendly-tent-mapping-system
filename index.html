Before you start

A few good questions.

Need help setting up your rental business?
Contact RentSketch →

Is RentSketch for businesses or individual events?

Both. Rental companies get a branded designer, catalog and business dashboard. Someone planning a single event can use the separate, one-time Event Pass.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Explore the interactive example without an account. Business workspaces include a 14-day trial with no credit card. The individual designer has a five-minute preview before an Event Pass is needed.

Can customers use it on my existing website?

Yes. Configure your branding and supported catalog, add your website domain in the Install screen, and copy the embed code or link to your designer.

Does it replace my booking system?

RentSketch focuses on visual plans and quote requests. Confirm equipment availability, delivery and booking details through your business workflow. It does not automatically synchronize with every rental system.

What happens when my business trial ends?

You are not charged automatically. Choose a subscription in Billing to continue. Your billing screen remains available after the trial expires.

How do I return to an event I purchased?

Use the private event link sent after payment, or visit Open my saved event to request another access email.
