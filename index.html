Before you start

A few good questions.

Need help setting up your rental business?
Contact RentSketch →

Is RentSketch for businesses or individual events?

Both. Rental companies get a branded designer, catalog and business dashboard. Someone planning a single event can use the separate, one-time Event Pass.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Explore the interactive example without an account. Business workspaces include a 14-day trial with no credit card. Individual event planners can watch the free demo before choosing a $9.99 Event Pass for paid editing.

What equipment can I add to a layout?

The visual library includes supported tents, round and banquet tables, folding and Chiavari chairs, dance floors, linens, lighting and inflatables. Business catalogs use supported visual mappings; a custom 3D model for every possible product is not included.

Can I print or share my event floor plan?

An Event Pass includes saving, printing and sharing one event during its access period. Rental businesses can use customer designs and quote requests in their dashboard. Keep your saved event’s private access link secure.

Does the 3D preview work on a phone?

The designer runs in a web browser. Interactive 3D requires a browser and device with WebGL support. The public wedding demo uses a lightweight staged overhead view on phones and reveals a rendered final reception; desktop browsers can load the interactive 3D scene after the first paint.

Can customers use it on my existing website?

Yes. Configure your branding and supported catalog, add your website domain in the Install screen, and copy the embed code or link to your designer.

Does it replace my booking system?

RentSketch focuses on visual plans and quote requests. Confirm equipment availability, delivery and booking details through your business workflow. It does not automatically synchronize with every rental system.

What happens when my business trial ends?

You are not charged automatically. Choose a subscription in Billing to continue. Your billing screen remains available after the trial expires.

How do I return to an event I purchased?

Use the private event link sent after payment, or visit Open my saved event to request another access email.
