Questions

RentSketch FAQ

What is RentSketch?

RentSketch is browser-based event layout software for planning tents, tables, chairs, dance floors, lighting and other supported event elements. The goal is to make event visualization accessible without requiring customers to learn traditional CAD software.

Can I use RentSketch to plan a tent layout?

Yes. RentSketch is designed around tent and event layouts, including seating and space-consuming additions such as dance floors. Final rental availability, installation requirements and site suitability should still be confirmed by the rental provider.

Does RentSketch have both 2D and 3D views?

Yes. The designer is built around one event scene that can be viewed as a 2D plan and in 3D.

Can a party rental company add RentSketch to its website?

Yes. The business version is designed for rental companies that want a branded event designer connected to their own website and configured around their rental offering.

Do customers need CAD experience?

No. RentSketch uses a guided planning flow so a customer can begin with familiar event questions before customizing the visual layout.

Does RentSketch replace my rental management software?

RentSketch is focused on event visualization and the design-to-request workflow. Your existing rental system can remain the source for confirmed inventory, contracts, payments and operational records.
