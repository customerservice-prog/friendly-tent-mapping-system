Start with the moments you are planning for.

Think about dinner, speeches, dancing and how guests arrive. Decide whether the ceremony shares the same space or uses a separate area. Those decisions determine what the reception layout needs to accommodate before you choose individual table positions.

Place the large areas first.

Begin with the tent or reception footprint. Add the dance floor, head table and service areas, then work through the guest tables. Make room for the equipment that is easy to overlook, such as a DJ area or a buffet table.

Review table shapes and the guest count together.

Round and banquet tables produce different arrangements. Choose the tables and chair counts that match the intended rental, and leave the space between them visible. The venue and rental team should review the plan against the real site and event requirements.

Share the picture, not only a list.

RentSketch lets you compare an overhead layout with a 3D view of the same event. Use the picture to discuss changes with the people helping you plan. The sample reception on our demo page gives you a way to explore the views before buying access.
