# Photo control interaction evidence

Generated by `tests/photo-controls-browser.cjs` against the production photo editor modules with Chromium 153. The colored image is a deterministic interaction fixture, not a customer venue, realistic rendering, or evidence of reconstructed depth.

- `photo-calibration-320.png`: 320px editor, distinct near/far corner labels. Transparent hit regions measure 44 × 44 CSS pixels regardless of image aspect ratio; visible dots stay 12px wide.
- `photo-foreground-390.png`: 390px foreground editor, selected-point menu for dense outlines and Add after point. Closely spaced vertices resolve to the nearest point instead of whichever SVG target is on top.
- `photo-foreground-desktop.png`: desktop foreground controls arranged in two columns to preserve photo space.

The test also passes at 390px and 1440px for calibration, and at 320px for foreground editing. Actual touch gestures start outside the visible dot and retain their drag offset. Repeated arrow and Shift-arrow nudges preserve keyboard focus and move in screen pixels. Resize and scene reopening preserve source-image coordinates and alignment. Dense tracing near the first point does not prematurely close an outline. Status text has stable height so feedback does not recenter the photo during a gesture.

These controls remain manual camera/foreground adjustments. They do not infer scale, build hidden geometry, or certify venue measurements.
