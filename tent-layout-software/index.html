Who is it for?

RentSketch is designed for party rental and event rental businesses that need a faster way to communicate tent layouts, as well as customers who want to understand what can fit before booking.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use RentSketch for party tent layouts?

Yes. The designer is built around event layouts and rental inventory, including tents and common items placed inside them.

Does it replace an engineering plan?

No. RentSketch is a visual event-planning tool. Structural engineering, permitting, staking, anchoring and installation requirements still need to follow the tent manufacturer's instructions and applicable local rules.

Can rental companies use it with customers?

Yes. RentSketch is intended to make customer layout conversations more visual and easier to understand.
