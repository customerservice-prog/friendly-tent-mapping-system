Will it work with my equipment?

The catalog supports dimensions, pricing and mappings to the visual library. Check your required equipment during the free trial; a custom 3D model for every product is not automatically included.

Will it automatically sync my rental system?

There is no automatic connection to every rental system. Embedding works independently; supported integrations and webhook workflows require configuration.

Can I collect rental deposits?

Deposit workflows require the appropriate business configuration and Stripe onboarding. A RentSketch software subscription is separate from your customers’ equipment payments.

How much is a business subscription?

Current plans are Starter $49/month, Pro $99/month and Business $199/month. Annual options are $490, $990 and $1,990. Compare plans.
