Will the business trial charge me automatically?

No. You do not enter a card to start. After 14 days, choose and purchase a subscription in your dashboard to continue.

When does a paid business plan start?

The subscription begins when you complete Stripe Checkout. The billing interval and total are shown before you pay. Monthly or annual subscriptions renew until canceled.

Can I cancel a business subscription?

Use Billing → Manage Billing in Stripe in your dashboard to manage payment details, invoices and cancellation. Check the effective date displayed by Stripe.

Does the Event Pass include rental equipment?

No. The Event Pass pays for software access to one design. Equipment, delivery, installation and the rental order are separate.

I already have a confirmed Friendly Party Rental booking. Do I pay again?

An eligible active confirmed Friendly booking includes design access. Use the existing order login with your first name and order number.

How do I get back to an event I paid for?

Payment returns you to the saved event. Your access email contains a private link, and Open my saved event can send it again. Keep the private link safe.
