Pricing questions

Do I need a subscription to design one event?

No. Event Pass is $9.99 for one event and gives that event 300 days of access. An additional 30 days is $4.99.

Do business plans require a credit card to start?

No. Business plans include a 14-day trial without requiring a credit card to begin.

Can I pay annually?

Yes. Starter is $490/year, Pro is $990/year and Business is $1,990/year.

What happens to Friendly Party Rental customers?

You can preview Friendly’s rentals for free. An Event Pass is $9.99 for 300 days of editing, saving, printing and sharing one event. It is not a rental deposit or a subscription.

How do I return after paying?

We email your private event link after payment. Open it on any device, or request your access email again. No password or code is needed.
